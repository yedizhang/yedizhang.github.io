Bio

I am a PhD candidate at the Gatsby Unit UCL, advised by Peter Latham and Andrew Saxe. I use theoretical approaches to study how neural networks with different architectures [ZSL26] learn, including attention-based [ZSLS25], fully-connected [ZSL25], and multimodal [ZLS24] networks.
